# Hypercare Plan

Status vocabulary source: [../StatusVocabulary.md](../StatusVocabulary.md)

## Hypercare Window

Duration: first two weeks after launch or pilot start.

## Objectives

1. Detect and resolve critical launch issues quickly.
2. Review operational health daily.
3. Capture customer and operator feedback.
4. Maintain evidence for release stabilization.
5. Decide when to exit hypercare.

## Issue Tracking

| Severity | Response Target | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Immediate triage; owner assigned within 15 minutes | Release owner and engineering lead |
| High | Owner assigned within 1 business hour | Operations owner |
| Medium | Owner assigned within 1 business day | Product/operations backlog |
| Low | Triage during daily review | Product backlog |

## Daily Health Review

| Review Item | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Uptime and monitoring status | Monitoring dashboard snapshot |
| Dispatch success and exceptions | Operations evidence |
| Emergency workflow health | Emergency dashboard evidence |
| Login/support issues | Support issue log |
| Customer feedback | Feedback summary |
| Open defects | Defect log |

## Customer Feedback

Capture:

1. Workflow friction.
2. Missing operational visibility.
3. Driver and dispatcher feedback.
4. Reporting and executive dashboard feedback.
5. Support incidents and response quality.

## Escalation Process

1. Critical issue reported.
2. Hypercare owner confirms severity.
3. Engineering and operations owners assigned.
4. Customer/support communication issued.
5. Resolution evidence captured.
6. Daily review confirms closure or mitigation.

## Exit Criteria

Hypercare can close when:

1. No critical defects are open.
2. No unresolved high defects remain without approved mitigation.
3. Monitoring and support process are stable.
4. Customer feedback has been reviewed and dispositioned.
5. Release board approves transition to standard operations.
