# Intelligence

## Modules

- delayDetector: identifies overdue pickup/drop-off and dwell risk.
- slaEngine: computes compliance, breaches, and warnings.
- riskEngine: computes operational risk with explainable reasons.

## Consumption

Intelligence signals feed:

- operations summaries
- automation escalation and recommendation paths
- executive and client confidence indicators

## Validation

Unit tests cover core calculations for:

- delay detection
- SLA summary
- risk scoring
